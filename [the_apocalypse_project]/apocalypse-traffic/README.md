# Apocalypse Traffic

Map-wide texture treatment for traffic lights, street lights and lighting poles.

The package covers 40 base street-light dictionaries and both primary traffic-light dictionaries. Metal housings and poles receive heavy oxidation, soot, streaking and surface fissures. Lower atlas regions receive denser grime and growth while lenses and normal maps remain functional.

This is a visual replacement only. Geometry, collisions, placements, signal logic and light behavior are unchanged.

## Testing

Restart the resource and reconnect before comparing close, medium and distant views. Inspect several city and county pole variants during both day and night. A useful traffic-light test point is `777.2983, 148.712357, 79.65961`.
