# Apocalypse Road Signs

Statewide texture-only weathering for GTA V road signs and related roadside fixtures.

The resource preserves the original wording, symbols, UV layouts, models and collisions. It streams weathered shared texture dictionaries and uses FiveM texture replacement for sign faces embedded in Rockstar model resources.

Treatment is intentionally severe and distance-readable: heavily faded reflective paint, broad edge corrosion, chipped faces, dark runoff, rusted backs and supports, and dense moss growth around lower edges and pole bases. The complete overhead freeway-guide family—including close, LOD and SLOD green destination boards, orange exit panels, lettering, borders, backs and supports—is included. Normal maps are left untouched.

The resource applies embedded roadside-sign replacements again after the player spawns and prints the applied replacement counts in F8. Four shared road dictionaries are merged into apocalypse-infrastructure to avoid competing streamed filenames.

Start or reload with:

```text
ensure apocalypse-road-signs
```

When already running:

```text
restart apocalypse-road-signs
```
