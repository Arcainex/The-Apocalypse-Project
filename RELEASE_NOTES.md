# v1.0.9 preview

This update gives more of the city the worn, abandoned look the project has been building towards. It also includes the v1.0.8 building material changes.

## Buildings

- Maze Tower now has deep impact openings, broken floors, interior walls, bent framing and rubble. Damaged office furniture and a few aircraft fragments sit inside the exposed areas.
- CNT has heavier facade damage, broken slabs and weathered lettering at the roof and street. The bright yellow finish has been removed.
- Fallen-FIB windows and nearby downtown facades have damaged materials, with matching changes at distance. The fallen-FIB structural rebuild is still being worked on and is not included here.
- Maze and CNT include updated collision and distance models. Keep `apocalypse-buildings` and `apocalypse-los-santos` on the same version.

## Streets and infrastructure

- More weathering across roads, pavements, kerbs, barriers, fuel stations, signs, banners, traffic lights and lamp posts.
- Smaller texture packages and independent road-texture loading to reduce streaming problems.
- PDM showroom state handling and additional IPL compatibility fixes.
- Cleanup of conflicting legacy map files and duplicate texture ownership.

## Before using it on a public server

This is a preview build. Offline asset checks have passed, but the new structural changes still need live FiveM collision, bullet, floor-contact, distance-transition and performance testing. Back up your current resources, update the matching map folders together and reconnect before comparing them.

The 100 m world-core pilot is included for review but remains disabled. New street placements, the six-building expansion and the fallen-FIB/Wiwang structural prototypes are not part of this release.
